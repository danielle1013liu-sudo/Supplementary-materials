Conceptual illustrations, not to scale or measured microstructures. Hydrogel composition represents polymer origin; bioactive particles symbolize material-associated functions and are not identified proteins or cytokines. Photo-crosslinking is one representative mechanism. Crosslink level is a categorical network-density proxy, not measured bond strength. Modulus includes different reported mechanical modulus types; the force arrow is illustrative. Pore size is the reported characteristic pore size, not molecular mesh size. The trees are a generic ensemble symbol, not the fitted tree topology. Mouse, skin and injection illustrate application context and do not establish in-vivo therapeutic efficacy.
